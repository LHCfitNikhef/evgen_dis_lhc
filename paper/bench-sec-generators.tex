\section{Monte Carlo event generators}
\label{sec:generators}

In this section we present the MC event generators that will be considered in this benchmark.
%
We focus on three general-purpose MC generators achieving NLO QCD accuracy, namely \sherpa, \herwig, and \powheg (both the V2 and RES variants). 
%
For inclusive observables, their predictions are compared with the analytical structure-function calculations provided by \yadism.
%
For completeness, we have also studied predictions provided by the LO MCs \pythia and \mgamc.
%
Furthermore, we consider event generators tailored to neutrino physics, with emphasis on \genie, the current default MC in FASER and SND@LHC, as well as {\sc\small NuWro} and {\sc\small GiBUU}.
%
Table~\ref{tab:generators} indicates the main features of these generators.
%
For each generator we indicate the version used, the source of the hard process, the QCD perturbative order, and the shower and hadronisation algorithms.
%
We provide now more details on each of these MC generators in turn.

\paragraph{\pythia.}  {\sc\small Pythia}~8.311~\cite{Bierlich:2022pfr} simulates DIS processes
through its internal LO neutral- and charged-current matrix elements, showered
with the $p_T$-ordered shower and hadronised with the Lund string model~\cite{Andersson:1983ia}.  
%
The baseline shower algorithm is taken to be the dipole-recoil scheme~\cite{Cabouat:2017rzi}, in which initial-state radiation recoils against the colour dipole rather than the scattered lepton.
%
In this work, \pythia is considered both as a stand-alone generator and as the provider of shower and hadronisation algorithms for some of the NLO generators.
%
Unless otherwise specified, here \pythia is run with the Monash tune~\cite{Skands:2014pea} for non-perturbative physics, other tunes such as the forward physics tune of~\cite{Fieg:2023kld} can be easily implemented. 

\paragraph{\sherpa.}  \sherpa~v3.0.5~\cite{Sherpa:2019gpd,Bothmann:2024} provides either an LO sample from {\sc Comix}~\cite{Gleisberg:2008fv} or an NLO sample using the MC@NLO~\cite{Frixione:2002ik} scheme for the matching to the Catani--Seymour dipole
shower~\cite{Schumann:2007mg}.
%
Hadronisation in \sherpa is based on the {\sc AHADIC} cluster model~\cite{Winter:2003tt,Chahal:2022rid}.  

\paragraph{\herwig.}  {\sc\small Herwig}~7.3.0~\cite{Bellm:2015jjp,Bewick:2023tfi} provides DIS simulations at LO through {\sc MEDISNC} and {\sc MEDISCC} modules, and at NLO through its POWHEG implementation matched to the angular-ordered shower.  Its cluster hadronisation~\cite{Webber:1983if} is implemented independently of {\sc AHADIC}.
%
\herwig admits two independent NLO implementations.  The first matches {\sc
Matchbox}~\cite{Platzer:2011bc} one-loop amplitudes to the shower in the MC@NLO scheme; the second is
\herwig's native POWHEG implementation, built on the same matrix-element classes as
the LO calculation. 
%
In this work, the second strategy is adopted for NLO event generation with \herwig.
%
The value of the intrinsic transverse
momentum used in these simulations is adjusted to match the DIS kinematics. 

\paragraph{\powheg.}  Two independent POWHEG~\cite{Nason:2004rx,Frixione:2007vw,Alioli:2010xd}
implementations are used and are distinguished throughout. 
%
First,
\powhegres is the
{\tt DIS\_v} process~\cite{Banfi:2023mhz} of POWHEG-BOX-RES~\cite{Jezo:2015aia}, covering inclusive and charm
production in both currents with mass effects neglected; it supplies the NLO
entry for neutral-current muon scattering. 
%
Second, \powhegtwo is the {\tt nu-DIS}
process of POWHEG-BOX-V2~\cite{Buonocore:2024pdv}, developed for neutrino DIS with a massive
final-state charm quark, and supplies the NLO entry for charged-current
neutrino scattering.  By default both are showered and hadronised with \pythia, and hence with
Lund-string hadronisation; where stated, their events are instead showered and hadronised with \herwig.
%
We note that mass effects in \powhegtwo are only available for the generation of charm-tagged events, while for inclusive DIS production charm quark mass effects are neglected. 
%
We note that \powhegres hardcodes a diagonal CKM matrix that never reaches its matrix elements, so
$d\to c$ is absent altogether: unitarity protects the inclusive CC rate but not the charm composition, and hence  it is quoted for the inclusive selection alone. 
%
For inclusive generation, both \powheg variants agree with each other and with \yadism at the per-cent level at NLO. 

\paragraph{\mgamc.}  \mgamc~3.7.2~\cite{Alwall:2014hca} is used to generate events at LO matched to \pythia.
%
In its current implementation, this generator cannot be used to simulate DIS events at NLO accuracy.

\paragraph{\genie.}  \genie~\cite{Andreopoulos:2009rq,Andreopoulos:2015wxa} is one of the most widely-used event generators for neutrino physics. 
%
As opposed to the other generators considered here, its settings (such as PDF choice or ME model) cannot be straightforwardly modified and are only available for a fixed set of tunes. 
%
Here LO simulations for neutrino scattering are produced with \genie using 
{\tt G18\_02a\_00\_000}~\cite{GENIE:2021zuu}, which is the same configuration that FASER uses~\cite{FASER:2024ykc}.\footnote{In the course of this work we found that the charm-production DIS cross-section of \genie multiplies by the $W$-boson propagator factor $(1+Q^2/M_W^2)$ instead of dividing by it. 
%
For $\nu_\mu$ scattering on a proton by about $4\%$ at $E_\nu=400$~GeV and $8\%$ at 1~TeV, growing rapidly at higher energies, while the fiducial inclusive cross-section changes by less than $0.2\%$. All \genie predictions in this work are obtained with the corrected cross-section; the \genie versions currently in use, including the one adopted by FASER, are affected.} 
%
This tune is built on the Bodek--Yang model~\cite{Bodek:2002vp,Bodek:2003wd} with
GRV98 LO parton distributions~\cite{Gluck:1998xa}.  For neutral-current muon scattering we use the
{\sc EMDIS} channel of that tune.
%
For CC scattering we also consider the {\sc HEDIS} module~\cite{Soto:2021vdc} of {\tt GHE19\_00a}, built on BGR18 structure functions~\cite{Bertone:2018dse} at NLO through {\sc APFEL}~\cite{Bertone:2013vaa}, achieving NLO accuracy for inclusive distributions.
%
This tune evaluates its structure functions with the {\tt NNPDF31sx\_nlo\_as\_0118\_LHCb\_nf\_6} set rather than with \nnpdf, a difference that turns out to account for the whole of its offset from our NLO reference, as discussed in Sect.~\ref{sec:inclusive}.
% 
To separate PDF effects from other model parameters, we also generate LO events where GRV98
is replaced by \nnpdf, both inside the Bodek--Yang parametrisation and inside
the separate charm-production algorithm.

\genie has no parton shower: the hadronic system is handed directly to the {\sc AGKY} model~\cite{Yang:2009zx}, which takes hadron multiplicities and momenta from an empirical Koba--Nielsen--Olesen parametrisation~\cite{Koba:1972ng} below a hadronic invariant mass of $2.3$~GeV and from \pythia string fragmentation above $3$~GeV, interpolating in between.
%
The fiducial region of this benchmark lies far above that window, so \genie final states are in practice Lund strings fragmented by \pythia, seeded by a different hard process and without perturbative radiation before fragmentation.
%
Here that fragmentation and the subsequent decays are carried out by \pythia rather than by the {\sc Pythia6} of the default configuration, and charm hadrons are decayed rather than left stable, so that the stability convention of Sect.~\ref{sec:settings} applies to \genie as to the other generators.

We note that \genie treats charm production as a separate process, with the Aivazis--Olness--Tung massive-charm cross-section~\cite{Aivazis:1993pi} and slow rescaling, and subtracts it from the inclusive deep-inelastic cross section
internally.  The charm channel must therefore be generated alongside the
inclusive one, as is relevant for instance for the first search for charm production in neutrino DIS at FASER$\nu$~\cite{FASER:2026charm}.
%
Concerning NC scattering, the {\sc EMDIS} channel
list is therefore a three-flavour calculation where charm is absent both from the initial and final state. 

A crucial advantage of \genie as compared to the general-purpose generators is that it can account also for non-DIS processes such as quasi-elastic, resonance, and diffractive scattering which are then combined with the DIS contribution.
%
Here we quantify the importance of these contributions for the FASER neutrino kinematics, finding that these effects can in general be neglected. 
%
Should it become necessary, DIS distributions simulated with other generators can be corrected for the non-DIS contributions as estimated by \genie. 

\paragraph{\yadism.} Analytic calculations of the DIS structure functions up to NNLO and accounting for heavy-quark mass effects are provided by
\yadism~\cite{Candido:2024rkr}, using the same PDFs, renormalisation and factorisation scales and electroweak inputs as the generators. 
%
Cross
sections are assembled from $F_2$, $F_L$ and $xF_3$ and then integrated over the
fiducial region.
%
\yadism further allows the heavy-quark scheme to be varied, comparing massless (ZM-VFNS) calculations with the FONLL matched ones~\cite{Forte:2010ta} which account for heavy quark mass effects.
%
\yadism has been benchmarked with other widely-used DIS tools such as \apfel~\cite{Bertone:2013vaa} and {\sc\small HOPPET}~\cite{Salam:2008qg}.
%
Although not considered here, \yadism enables also the calculation of DIS structure functions at (partial) N$^3$LO accuracy~\cite{NNPDF:2024nan}.
